My Favorite Series: Squid Game

About the Series
My favorite Netflix series is Squid Game. It is a Korean survival drama about people who join dangerous games to win a large amount of money.

Genre
- Thriller
- Drama
- Survival
- Mystery

Why I Like It
1. The story is interesting.
2. The games are exciting.
3. The characters have different stories.
4. Every episode has unexpected twists.

Favorite Characters
- Seong Gi-hun
- Kang Sae-byeok
- Cho Sang-woo
- Hwang Jun-ho

My Rating
9/10

Squid Game shows how people make difficult choices when their lives are at risk.
I like this series because it is exciting and keeps me curious about what will happen next.
